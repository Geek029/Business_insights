# Business Insights - Power BI dashboards for decision makers.
Dashboards built for finance, sales, marketing and supply chain teams to help
them with everyday decisions.

### Financial Analysis:
- Tracking key Financial KPIs
- Financial performance analysis
- Regional performance analysis

### Sales Analysis:
- Customer performance
- Segment sales analysis

### Marketing Analysis:
- Marketing spend analysis
- Margins per region

### Supply chain analysis:
- Forecasting accuracy
- Inventory management

### Executive view:
- KPI's, Market share
- Customer and market performance
